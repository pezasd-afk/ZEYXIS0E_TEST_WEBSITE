Location

Why
Kyparissi?

Kyparissi, Lakonia is a hidden coastal gem of the Peloponnese, where crystal-clear waters, dramatic mountain views, and authentic Greek village life come together. Peaceful, private, and untouched by mass tourism, it offers the kind of natural beauty and calm atmosphere that is becoming increasingly rare.

From its charming traditional village to the seaside area of Paralia, Kyparissi is surrounded by beautiful beaches, local tavernas, olive trees, pine forests, walking trails, and open views of the Myrtoan Sea. It is an ideal destination for those looking for a holiday home or permanent retreat in a place that feels both exclusive and genuinely authentic.

Our current development is located in Kyparissi, Lakonia, in the traditional settlement of Vrysi, approximately 700 metres from the sea. The project consists of two newly constructed maisonette-style residences currently under development.

The location combines the natural beauty of the Parnonas mountain landscape with easy access to the sea - a unique setting for both summer and year-round living. Each residence includes living areas, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, verandas, private parking, outdoor shower, and BBQ area, with independent systems for heating, cooling, water, electricity, and security.
